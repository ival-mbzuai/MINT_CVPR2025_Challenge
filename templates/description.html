Task

Multilingual Video Reasoning Evaluation challenge proposes a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate the multilingual and culturally grounded reasoning capabilities of Video Large Multimodal Models (Video-LMMs). Going beyond standard benchmarks that typically focus on English and generic video understanding, it enables rigorous testing across a broad linguistic and cultural spectrum by incorporating 14 diverse languages—including several under-represented ones—and 15 real-world domains. The languages involve Arabic, Bengali, Chinese, English, French, German, Hindi, Japanese, Russian, Sinhala, Spanish, Swedish, Tamil and Urdu.

Dataset

The validation split consists of 1440 open-ended and 1180 mcq based question-answer (QA) pairs spanning 354 unique videos. As for the test split it consists of 2039 open-ended and 3366 QA pairs, derived from 525 unique videos. In overall, the dataset spans 14 languages with total of 8025 QA pairs from 879 videos, where they belong to 7 generic categories and 8 cultural categories.

Challenge Phases

  1. Validation Phase: Participants submit their model predictions on the validation dataset. Participants can choose to make submissions public or private during this phase and the number of submissions are limited to 5 per day and 30 per month.
  2. Test Phase: Participants submit their model predictions on the test dataset. The final evaluation and ranking will be based on the performance on this split. The top-performing models will be awarded. Submissions are limited to 20 per day and 200 per month.

Competition Rules

  1. Submissions must be public in the test phase to be considered for the prize.
  2. Participants need to submit a technical report (PDF file).
  3. There must be no explicit human labelling on the test videos.
  4. Test and validation data must not be used for any kind of training (supervised or self-supervised). Any other dataset can be used for training, fine-tuning, or prompting.
  5. There are no restrictions on the model side; any model is eligible for prizes.
