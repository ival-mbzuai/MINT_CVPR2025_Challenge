Competition Introduction

Welcome to the Multilingual Video Reasoning Challenge at CVPR 2025, hosted by MBZUAI-IVAL. This challenge aims to evaluate model’s ability to reason over videos in diverse linguistic and cultural contexts, pushing the boundaries of multilingual video understanding.

Our dataset spans 11 languages and includes a rich collection of videos with corresponding question-answer (QA) pairs, covering both open-ended and multiple-choice (MCQ) formats. The dataset is structured into 7 diverse categories, with a dedicated Cultural category that features multiple sub-categories, capturing the complexity of cross-cultural understanding.

Participants will work with test and validation sets designed to assess generalization across languages, question types, and video content. This challenge offers a unique opportunity to advance the development of robust multimodal AI models capable of understanding video content beyond English-centric datasets.

Join us in pushing the limits of multilingual and cross-cultural video reasoning!
